SECTION 2. TECHNICAL DESCRIPTION OF THE INNOVATION
================================================================================
The Quantum Widget addresses a critical physical bottleneck in current fault-tolerant quantum computing systems: rapid thermal decoherence in high-density qubit arrays. Operational quantum logic circuits require near-absolute zero ambient conditions to preserve quantum entanglement states and mitigate thermal excitation errors across superconducting qubit junctions.

The present invention overcomes existing thermal management limitations through a multi-stage cryogenic dissipation architecture. Specifically, the core innovation relies on sub-zero cooling coils constructed from a non-magnetic micro-porous ceramic composite. These sub-zero cooling coils are disposed in direct thermal contact with the quantum processor substrate and circulate a pressurized multi-isotope liquid helium mixture operating at sub-Kelvin temperatures (between 0.05 K and 1.5 K).

The helical configuration of the sub-zero cooling coils establishes continuous micro-fluidic turbulence, eliminating localized thermal hot spots without generating micro-vibrations or electromagnetic interference that could disrupt qubit state coherence.

SECTION 3. CLAIMS OVERVIEW & SCOPE OF PROTECTION
================================================================================
Patent Application #US-998877 contains twenty (20) pending claims, structured into three (3) primary independent claims and seventeen (17) narrow dependent claims designed to withstand re-examination challenges:

3.1 Independent Claim 1 (Apparatus):
"A quantum processing system comprising: a quantum logic substrate having a plurality of superconducting qubits; a vacuum enclosure surrounding said substrate; and sub-zero cooling coils positioned adjacent to said substrate, wherein said sub-zero cooling coils circulate a cryogenic fluid at temperatures below 2.0 Kelvin to dissipate thermal energy during quantum computation execution."

3.2 Dependent Claim 2 (Helical Coil Geometry):
"The system of Claim 1, wherein the sub-zero cooling coils comprise a dual-helical counter-flow configuration arranged to maintain uniform thermal gradient distribution across the entire surface area of said quantum logic substrate."

3.3 Dependent Claim 5 (Fluid Composition):
"The system of Claim 1, wherein the cryogenic fluid circulating within the sub-zero cooling coils consists of a diluted Helium-3 / Helium-4 mixture maintained at a constant fluid pressure between 1.2 bar and 2.5 bar."

3.4 Independent Claim 12 (Method Claim):
"A method for preserving quantum entanglement coherence in a quantum processor, comprising circulating cryogenic coolant through sub-zero cooling coils surrounding a qubit array during active gate operations."
