# Schedule 2 — Service Levels

## Service tiers

### Tier: @@struct-band@@

Applies to incidents raised by the Customer's nominated contacts against
production environments.

- Response targets
  - Priority 1
    - Acknowledgement: within one hour
    - Service credit per breached target: @@struct-leaf@@
  - Priority 2
    - Acknowledgement: within four hours

Applies to all other environments.

- Response targets
  - Priority 1
