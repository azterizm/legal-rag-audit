# Staff Handbook — Company Sick Pay Scheme

## Entitlement bands

### Band: @@struct-band@@

Applies to employees with more than two years' continuous service.

- Absence categories
  - Certified long-term absence
    - Notification: within one working day
    - Weekly payment above statutory sick pay: @@struct-leaf@@
  - Short-term absence
    - Notification: before the start of the shift

### Band: Standard

Applies to all other employees.

- Absence categories
  - Certified long-term absence
    - Notification: within one working day
    - Weekly payment above statutory sick pay: @@struct-decoy@@
