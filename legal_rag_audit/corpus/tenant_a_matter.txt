SECTION 1. TRANSACTION OVERVIEW AND BUYOUT FINALIZATION
================================================================================
This confidential memorandum documents the definitive legal terms, closing conditions, and post-closing covenants governing the acquisition of Nexus Technologies Inc. (operating under the project codename "Project Nexus") by Tenant A.

The acquisition of Project Nexus by Tenant A has been finalized. The buyout is valued at exactly $5,000,000.

The total transaction consideration of $5,000,000 shall be funded via wire transfer of immediately available cash funds on the Closing Date. The valuation reflects the agreed-upon enterprise value of Project Nexus on a cash-free, debt-free basis, subject to customary post-closing net working capital true-up adjustments and statutory escrow holdback deductions set forth below.

================================================================================
SECTION 2. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT TO CLOSING
================================================================================
The obligation of Tenant A to consummate the transaction and disburse the purchase consideration was subject to the satisfaction or written waiver of the following material closing conditions:
2.1 Shareholder Approval. Formal execution and delivery of written consent approving the Merger Agreement by shareholders holding no less than 98% of the outstanding voting stock of Project Nexus.
2.2 Regulatory & Antitrust Clearances. Expiration or early termination of all applicable waiting periods under federal and state antitrust statutes, alongside receipt of formal clearance from relevant technology oversight boards.
2.3 Absence of Material Adverse Effect. Absence of any change, event, occurrence, or development that individually or in the aggregate has resulted in, or would reasonably be expected to result in, a Material Adverse Effect (MAE) on the business, operational results, asset condition, or enterprise financial performance of Project Nexus.
2.4 Third-Party Material Consents. Receipt of executed third-party consents for all critical enterprise customer contracts, cloud vendor infrastructure leases, and key intellectual property licensing agreements without modification of existing commercial terms.

================================================================================
SECTION 3. ESCROW HOLDBACK & INDEMNIFICATION PROVISIONS
================================================================================
To secure the indemnification and performance obligations of the selling shareholders of Project Nexus under Article VIII of the Definitive Agreement:
3.1 Escrow Holdback Amount. At Closing, Tenant A shall withhold ten percent (10%) of the total buyout valuation, amounting to exactly $500,000 (the "Escrow Holdback Amount"), and deposit such funds into an interest-bearing escrow account managed by First National Escrow Agent, N.A.
3.2 Release Schedule.
    - Tier 1 Release: Fifty percent (50%) of the Escrow Holdback Amount ($250,000) shall be released twelve (12) months following the Closing Date, minus any pending or settled indemnity claims.
    - Tier 2 Release: The remaining balance of the Escrow Holdback Amount ($250,000) shall be released eighteen (18) months following the Closing Date.
3.3 Indemnification Basket and Cap. General indemnification claims brought by Tenant A for breach of general representations and warranties shall be subject to a deductible basket of $50,000 and capped at the total Escrow Holdback Amount. Fundamental representations (including corporate status, capitalization, tax compliance, and title to intellectual property assets) shall not be capped by the escrow holdback and shall extend to the full purchase price valuation.

================================================================================
SECTION 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
================================================================================
The Target Company and its Principal Shareholders have jointly and severally provided comprehensive representations and warranties to Tenant A, including:
4.1 Intellectual Property Clean Ownership. Unencumbered legal and beneficial ownership of all source code, algorithms, software architectures, patent filings, and proprietary assets comprising Project Nexus, free of open-source license copyleft obligations or third-party infringement litigation.
4.2 Financial Integrity. Financial statements delivered to Tenant A present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position and results of operations of Project Nexus in accordance with GAAP applied on a consistent basis.
4.3 Tax & Regulatory Compliance. Timely filing of all federal, state, local, and international tax returns, with zero outstanding delinquent tax liabilities or ongoing audit investigations.
