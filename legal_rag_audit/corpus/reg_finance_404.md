# Financial Regulation 404

## 1. Compliance Tiers
- **Tier 1**: Assets < $10M
  - Must report annually.
- **Tier 2**: Assets >= $10M
  - Must report quarterly.
  - Sub-requirement: Must retain external auditor.

## 2. Penalty Schedule
| Tier | Offense | Fine |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Late Filing | $5,000 |
| Tier 2 | Late Filing | $50,000 |

*Note to Section 1 Tier 2: The external auditor must be rotated every 4 years.*
